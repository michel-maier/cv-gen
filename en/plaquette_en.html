Three sentences I hear before every engagement

“Nobody knows how it works any more.”

The original team has gone, there are no tests and the documentation is patchy, and every fix breaks something else. I map the system and put a safety net of tests over the critical paths before touching anything.

“We are being told to rewrite everything.”

It is the riskiest project a company can start: double the budget, two years without a new feature, and the old system still running. I break the existing one down piece by piece, each step shippable and reversible.

“We have been talking about AI for two years, nothing is live.”

Because it is treated as a demo instead of an engineering problem. I embed models in the product, with the reviews and the tests that go with them. And if your data must not leave your walls, they run inside them.
